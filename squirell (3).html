Core features

Everything a family needs to build a saving habit.

Eight connected tools. One calm, trustworthy experience for parents, children, teenagers and young adults.

01

Goal-Based Savings

Create goals for products, education, experiences and future needs. Contribute any amount, whenever you want — no mandatory daily or monthly deposits.

02

Smart Savings Lock

Savings stay committed until the target is reached. Proposed 1.5% break-lock fee, with emergency exceptions and transparent terms.

03

Squirell RoundUp

Pay ₹156, save ₹4. Spare change becomes progress, with approval or authorised automation.

04

Squirell UPI

Scan QR, send and receive money, view history — with savings prompts built in.

05

Debit Card

Physical and virtual cards with limits, instant alerts and parental controls.

06

Family Savings

Parents, grandparents and relatives contribute through shared goals and Gift a Goal links.

07

Marketplace

Target achieved? Buy through Squirell or withdraw eligible funds and buy independently.

08

Financial Literacy & Rewards

Milestones, streaks, achievements and bite-sized financial education that make responsible money habits feel rewarding.
